Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · PixelMind AI

Short version: PixelMind AI does not collect, store, or sell your personal data. Everything stays on your device. We only use third-party services to generate images and display ads.

1 Who We Are

PixelMind AI is a free web application available at pixel-mind-amber.vercel.app. It allows users to generate images from text prompts using publicly available AI APIs. The app is provided free of charge and is supported by advertising.

2 Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or process any of the following:

  • Your name, email, or any account information (no login required)
  • Your text prompts or generated images — these are processed directly by third-party AI APIs
  • Your device location
  • Your IP address or browsing history
  • Any payment information
3 Data Stored on YOUR Device Only

The following data is stored locally in your browser's localStorage and never leaves your device:

  • Generated image history — your gallery of created images (stored as base64 in your browser)
  • API keys — if you choose to add HuggingFace, Together.ai, or Stability AI keys
  • Usage limit counter — tracks your 20 generations per 12-hour window
  • App preferences — selected model, style, and ratio settings

You can clear all of this at any time via Settings → Clear Gallery or by clearing your browser data.

4 Third-Party AI Services

When you generate an image, your text prompt is sent to one of these third-party APIs:

  • Pollinations.ai — primary image generation (free, no key required)
  • HuggingFace — fallback generation service
  • Together.ai — fallback generation service
  • Stability AI — fallback generation service

These services have their own privacy policies. By using PixelMind AI, you agree that your prompts are sent to these services for processing. We recommend not entering sensitive personal information in your prompts.

5 Advertising

PixelMind AI displays advertisements provided by Monetag to keep the service free. Monetag may use cookies and similar technologies to serve relevant ads. This may include:

  • Anonymous usage data for ad targeting
  • Cookie-based tracking for ad frequency management

Monetag's data collection is governed by their own privacy policy at monetag.com/privacy-policy. You can opt out of personalised ads through your browser settings or by visiting your device's ad settings.

6 Cookies

PixelMind AI itself does not set cookies. However, our advertising partner Monetag may set cookies for ad delivery and frequency capping. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

7 Children's Privacy

PixelMind AI is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has used our service, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of PixelMind AI after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

9 Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is no data to request, modify, or delete from our end. All your data is stored locally on your device and you are in full control of it. You can delete it anytime by clearing your browser storage.

Questions or Concerns?

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, feel free to reach out.
